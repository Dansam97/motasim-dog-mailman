One funny one-line review a dog gives the mailman, under 200 characters — a rating, a grievance, and a promise, all in the dog's own voice. The entry was picked from a five-candidate tournament judged on the kit's criteria: the dog's side of the visit as the joke, a dog's priorities and vocabulary, a tight line with a turn at the end, and a laugh specific to this dog and this mailman.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
